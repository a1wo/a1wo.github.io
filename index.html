Continuous Control of Editing Models via Adaptive-Origin Guidance

Alon Wolf1,2, Chen Katzir2, Kfir Aberman2, Or Patashnik1

1Tel Aviv University   2Decart.ai

We introduce AdaOr (Adaptive-Origin Guidance), enabling smooth and continuous control over edit intensity in diffusion-based image and video editing models — without per-edit optimization or specialized datasets. By introducing a learnable identity instruction and interpolating the CFG origin, AdaOr allows seamless linear interpolation between the input and any target edit.
